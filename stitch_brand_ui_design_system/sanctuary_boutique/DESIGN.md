## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for an intimate, automated boutique hospitality experience in Phu Quoc. Centered around the core promise of absolute discretion ("Riêng Tư Tuyệt Đối"), the design language balances quiet luxury with high-efficiency mobile commerce. 

### Brand Personality & Core Audience
- **Target Audience:** Independent travelers and couples aged 25–35 seeking swift, private, and frictionless stays with zero front-desk friction.
- **Brand Traits:** Discreet, reassuring, serene, warm, and hyper-reliable.
- **Emotional Response:** An instant sense of decompression, security, and effortless autonomy. The digital experience acts as an invisible host that respects the guest's privacy.

### Design Movement: Modern Coastal Editorial
The aesthetic synthesizes modern minimalism with warm coastal hospitality. Structural deep navy foundations ground the interface, elevated by soft warm gold accents that guide conversion paths without visual clutter. Typography pairs a refined transitional serif for expressive relaxation with a crisp, geometric sans-serif engineered for high legibility under sunlight and fast thumb navigation.

## Colors

The palette relies on strict functional segmentation: structural prestige, golden conversion, crisp neutrals, and unequivocal operational states.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1B3A4B`):** Deep oceanic navy. Used for structural hierarchy—sticky header navigation, footer canvases, dark immersive hero layers, major section headers, and brand identification.
- **Secondary / CTA (`#D4A853`):** Warm burnished gold. Reserved strictly for primary action elements, checkout buttons, pricing callouts, and key highlight badges.
- **Secondary Hover (`#C09540`):** Rich antique gold. Applied to buttons and interactive cards upon hover/active taps.
- **Tertiary / Success (`#10B981`):** Calibrated emerald green. Communicates operational vacancy ("Phòng trống"), successful VietQR payment capture, and verified verification states.
- **Neutral Primary (`#2D2D2D`):** Deep charcoal for optimal reading comfort on mobile screens without the harshness of pure black.
- **Neutral Muted (`#6B7280`):** Medium slate gray for form placeholders, metadata labels, and tertiary annotations.
- **Backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` & `#F7F7F7`):** Pure white canvas for interactive cards and primary forms, transitioning into subtle neutral gray for section banding, gallery backdrops, and administrative containers.
- **Status Danger (`#EF4444`):** Pure alert crimson for room occupancy locks ("Phòng bận"), conflict notices, and critical destructive actions.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy pairs architectural grace with ergonomic utility:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes resort relaxation, warmth, and distinguished hospitality. Reserved for hero statements, room names, and primary section banners.
- **Functional Interface (`Inter`):** Mobile-first sans-serif for UI interactions, booking configurations, guest inputs, pricing breakouts, and long-form information.
- **Physical Access & Tokens (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated monospace styling utilized exclusively for Smart Pass access keys, door PINs, and transaction identifiers, maximizing readability at night and under low-contrast scenarios.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point modular spacing grid with dynamic fluid adjustments tailored for one-handed mobile interactions.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Columns
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** 4-column layout, edge margins of `1rem`, element vertical gap of `1rem`. Full-width touch cards, vertically stacked booking bar, sticky bottom CTA bar on active steps.
- **Tablet (`640px – 1024px`):** 8-column layout, edge margins of `1.5rem`, gutters of `1.25rem`. 2-column room listings and split-pane summary layouts.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column layout, maximum content container width of `1200px` centered, edge margins of `2.5rem`, gutters of `1.5rem`.

### Overlap & Flow Dynamics
- **Quick Booking Floating Panel:** Positioned with a negative top margin (`-2.5rem`) overlapping the base of the Hero section, visually bridging atmospheric photography with transactional clarity.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Page sections maintain consistent padding (`space-xl` on mobile, doubled on desktop) to foster an unhurried, calm visual tone.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean planar stacking paired with soft ambient shadows to keep the interface light and breathable.

### Shadow Styles
- **Subtle Surface (`elevation-low`):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(27, 58, 75, 0.06), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(27, 58, 75, 0.04)`. Applied to standard room cards, amenity chips, and input fields.
- **Floating Overlap (`elevation-mid`):** `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(27, 58, 75, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 58, 75, 0.06)`. Applied to the Quick Booking Bar, dropdown select menus, and sticky navigation headers.
- **Modal & Smart Pass (`elevation-high`):** `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(27, 58, 75, 0.20), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(27, 58, 75, 0.08)`. Applied to the QR payment modal, booking confirmation sheets, and the Smart Pass digital ticket.

### Atmosphere & Overlays
Hero photography employs an ambient dark blue scrim gradient (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(27, 58, 75, 0.3) 0%, rgba(27, 58, 75, 0.8) 100%)`) to guarantee high-contrast legibility for white display serif titles.

## Shapes

The design system utilizes rounded shapes (`roundedness: 2`) that evoke comfort, safety, and modern hospitality.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Small Elements (Badges, Amenity Tags, Checkboxes):** `0.375rem` to `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`).
- **Interactive Inputs & Primary Buttons:** `0.75rem` (`rounded-lg`) to `1rem` (`rounded-xl`), providing comfortable touch targets.
- **Surface Cards (Room Cards, Booking Summary, Admin Cards):** `1rem` (`rounded-xl`) to `1.5rem` (`rounded-2xl`).
- **Smart Pass Key Card:** `1.5rem` (`rounded-2xl`) featuring scalloped concave ticket cutouts along its lateral separator line.
- **Pill Accents:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full`) reserved strictly for status pills (`Phòng trống` / `Phòng bận`) and the floating mobile call-to-action button.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary CTA ("Đặt Ngay" / "Xác Nhận"):** Background `#D4A853`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Inter` SemiBold (`label-md`), height `48px` (mobile touch target), corner radius `rounded-xl`. Transitions on hover to `#C09540` with a subtle elevation shift.
- **Secondary Action ("Khám Phá Phòng"):** Transparent fill, 1.5px solid border in `#FFFFFF` (on hero) or `#1B3A4B` (on light surfaces), text matching border color.
- **Icon Buttons:** `40px x 40px` circular or rounded-lg containers with centered line icons, padded with `0.5rem`.

### 2. Room Card
- **Structure:** Vertical card featuring an image carousel/thumbnail with a `16:10` aspect ratio at the top, followed by a body container (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Badges:** Floating top-right status pill (`#10B981` background with white text for available; `#EF4444` background with white text for occupied).
- **Amenities Row:** Subtle horizontal chips with neutral background `#F7F7F7`, small icon, and text in `#6B7280` (`body-sm`).
- **Footer:** Prominent pricing in warm gold (`#D4A853`, `headline-sm`) juxtaposed with a compact Primary CTA button.

### 3. Smart Pass Digital Key Card
- **Structure:** Elevated card (`elevation-high`) featuring a navy header (`#1B3A4B`) and crisp white credentials body.
- **Header:** House icon, Room Name (e.g., "PHÒNG 102"), and booking period.
- **Divider:** Subtle horizontal dashed border with lateral semi-circle ticket notches.
- **PIN Display:** JetBrains Mono font (`code-ticket`), centered, high-contrast `#1B3A4B` on a `#F7F7F7` padded capsule, accompanied by a quick "Sao Chép" (Copy) button.
- **Real-Time Badge:** SSE-connected indicator showing "Mã mở khóa khả dụng" in `#10B981`.

### 4. 7-Step Booking Indicator
- **Progress Track:** Lightweight horizontal tracker positioned at the top of the booking funnel.
- **Steps:** Numbered circular nodes (`28px`). Completed steps are filled with `#1B3A4B` and display a check icon; the active step has a `#D4A853` border and gold fill; upcoming steps are muted neutral gray `#E5E7EB`.
- **Labels:** On mobile, only the active step label is rendered in bold text below the tracker to preserve viewport width.

### 5. Input Fields & Selectors
- **States:** Height `48px`, border 1px solid `#E5E7EB`, surface `#FFFFFF`, text `#2D2D2D`.
- **Focus:** 2px border in `#1B3A4B` with zero harsh box-shadow ring.
- **Date/Time Combined Picker:** Large clickable surface displaying calendar/clock icons with clear, legible values formatted for Vietnamese locale conventions (`DD/MM/YYYY`, `HH:mm`).

### 6. Quick Booking Bar
- **Surface:** Solid `#FFFFFF` container with `elevation-mid` and `rounded-2xl` styling.
- **Mobile Reflow:** Vertical stack with `space-sm` separation between Date, Time, Room Type, and the gold submit button.
- **Desktop Reflow:** 4-segment inline horizontal bar with subtle vertical dividers between input segments.

### 7. Admin Components
- **Metric Cards:** `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-xl`, subtle border in `#F3F4F6`. Features large numbers in `Playfair Display` or bold `Inter`, paired with small positive trend indicators in `#10B981`.
- **Room Grid Switcher:** 4-column real-time room overview displaying current occupancy, instant lock/unlock toggle, and current active door PIN code.
